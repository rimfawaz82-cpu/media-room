# Phase 6 — Robustness of the two candidates (DEV + VAL only; holdout still untouched)

_Generated by `src/robust.py`, `src/swing.py`, `src/p6_report.py`. Frozen rules: `rules/xae_swing_v1.json`._

## 0. Plain-language summary

* **Swing candidate (Donchian-20, long only) is robust within the tested sample.** 98% of 84 neighbouring settings (entry 10–55 days, exit 3–55 days, 4 stop types) are profitable; it barely changes with doubled costs, a one-day delay or higher swap; with the **real CFI swap** it makes 8.0%/yr, max drawdown 11.7%, Sharpe 0.84. Monte Carlo: 5th–95th percentile annual return 1.5% → 15.2%, 95th-percentile drawdown 19.5%.
* Caveat that robustness checks cannot remove: the whole sample is a gold bull market and the system is long-only. Whether it adds value **beyond being long gold** is only suggestive (p ≈ 0.06 vs random timing). The holdout (2025-07 → 2026-11) contains a +40% rally *and* a −28% fall, so it is a fair test.
* **Intraday candidate (NY ORB on high-volatility days) is NOT robust:** only the 30-minute opening range works; 15, 20 and 45 minutes are ≈ 0. With CFI costs only 33% of variants are positive on validation and 33% in 2025; under C2 nearly all lose. A result that exists only at one parameter value is a fitting artefact. **Rejected.**
