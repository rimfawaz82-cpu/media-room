## 0. Plain-language summary

* **Swing (multi-day) track:** of 12 classic trend systems, the best was **20-day Donchian breakout, long only**: +8.2% per year on unlevered notional, max drawdown 11.7%, Sharpe 0.86, no losing year — while invested only 31% of the time. Buy-and-hold made +11.9%/yr with a 22.3% drawdown (Sharpe 0.72).
* But it is **only 21 trades**, and against random long trades of the same number and length its result has p = 0.064 — suggestive, **not proof**, especially as the best of 12 systems.
* **Every short side lost** (gold rose ≈1.8× from Dec-2020 to Jun-2025). Long/short trend systems are not supported by this sample.
* **Intraday track:** the NY opening-range breakout traded **only on high-volatility days** passed the development screen (+0.130 R, t = 2.21) but was flat on validation (+0.011 R) and negative in 2025 → **weak candidate, not accepted.** Compression breakouts and swing-aligned intraday entries showed no edge.
* Configurations tested in total so far: ≈ 111.

## 1. Swing systems — setup

* Daily series 2020-01 → 2025-06 (holdout excluded): D1 segment 2020-01-02 → 2021-11-15, H1-aggregated → 2022-03-15, M1-aggregated → 2025-06-30. Evaluation starts 2020-12-01 (indicator warm-up); "2020" in the tables is therefore only December.
* Execution at the next day's open with $0.50 per side (wide reopen spread + slippage); stops on daily high/low, gaps fill at the open; financing (swap) scenarios W0 = 0, **W1 = 4%/yr (base)**, W2 = 8%/yr, charged on long and short positions. **The broker's real swap rates are unknown and matter a lot for swing trading.**
* Systems (classic defaults, not optimised): Donchian 20/55 (exit on the opposite N/2 channel, 2-ATR initial stop with ATR trail), EMA 20/50 and 50/200 crossover (3-ATR chandelier stop; after a stop, flat until the next cross), time-series momentum 20/60 days (weekly rebalance, no stop). LS = long & short, LO = long only.
